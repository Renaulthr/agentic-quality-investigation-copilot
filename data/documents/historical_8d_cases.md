# Historical 8D Cases

## Case H8D-001
Issue:
Compression ring broken during internal inspection.

Evidence:
- High assembly force observed
- Ring mounting tool wear identified

Confirmed Cause:
Worn mounting tool caused excessive ring stress.

Corrective Action:
Tool replaced and preventive inspection frequency increased.


## Case H8D-002
Issue:
Compression ring broken at customer end.

Evidence:
- Internal inspection records were OK
- Assembly force within normal range
- Retained samples OK
- Packaging damage observed

Investigation Conclusion:
Post-inspection handling or transport damage considered most likely.

Corrective Action:
Packaging reinforcement and handling review.


## Case H8D-003
Issue:
Circlip missing.

Evidence:
