# Inspection Standard – Piston Assembly

## Ring Inspection

Check:
- Ring presence
- Ring damage
- Ring orientation
- Correct groove placement

Acceptance:
All required rings must be present and visually undamaged.

## Circlip Inspection

Check:
- Circlip presence
- Correct seating
- No visible deformation

Acceptance:
Both required circlips must be correctly installed.

## Final Assembly Inspection

Check:
- Complete assembly
- No visible damage
- No loose component
- Correct identification

Disposition:
OK → Release
NG → Hold and segregate
