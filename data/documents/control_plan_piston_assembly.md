# Control Plan – Piston Assembly

## Ring Assembly

Characteristic:
Ring presence and correct sequence

Control Method:
- Camera inspection
- Sensor confirmation
- Operator visual check

Frequency:
100% inspection

Reaction Plan:
- Stop process
- Segregate suspected assemblies
- Verify last accepted piece
- Resume only after process confirmation


## Circlip Assembly

Characteristic:
Circlip presence

Control Method:
- Sensor detection
- Pressure monitoring
- Scanner verification

Frequency:
100%

Reaction Plan:
- Stop process
- Segregate affected parts
- Verify sensor and feeding condition


## Final Inspection

Characteristic:
Complete assembly condition

Control Method:
100% visual inspection

Reaction Plan:
- Hold NG part
- Verify preceding production
- Escalate abnormal trend to quality
