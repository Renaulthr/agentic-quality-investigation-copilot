# PFMEA – Piston Assembly Process

## Process: Ring Assembly

### Failure Mode: Compression Ring Missing
Potential Effect:
- Incomplete piston assembly
- Engine performance concern
- Customer rejection

Potential Causes:
- Ring feeding failure
- Incorrect manual loading
- Sensor bypass
- Ring mounting sequence interruption

Current Controls:
- Ring presence verification
- Camera inspection
- Final visual inspection

Recommended Action:
- Verify sensor response
- Review ring feeder condition
- Confirm sequence poka-yoke status


### Failure Mode: Compression Ring Broken

Potential Effect:
- Reduced sealing performance
- Customer complaint
- Engine performance concern

Potential Causes:
- Excessive assembly force
- Incorrect ring mounting
- Ring handling damage
- Post-inspection handling damage

Current Controls:
- Assembly force monitoring
- Visual inspection
- Camera-based presence verification
- Final inspection

Recommended Action:
- Review assembly force trend
- Inspect retained sample
- Check ring loading condition
- Review downstream handling


## Process: Circlip Assembly

### Failure Mode: Circlip Missing

Potential Effect:
- Pin retention failure
- Severe customer quality concern

Potential Causes:
- Feeding interruption
- Sensor failure
- Incorrect manual intervention

Current Controls:
- Circlip presence detection
- Pressure monitoring
- Scanner verification
- Final inspection

Recommended Action:
- Verify presence sensor
- Review pressure trace
- Inspect feeding mechanism
