# Work Instruction – Ring Assembly

## Objective

Ensure correct mounting of piston rings without damage.

## Procedure

1. Confirm correct piston model.
2. Verify ring set availability.
3. Inspect ring condition before loading.
4. Load rings according to defined sequence.
5. Confirm mounting tool condition.
6. Perform ring assembly.
7. Verify ring presence using inspection system.
8. Transfer assembly only after OK confirmation.

## Abnormal Conditions

Stop production if:
- Ring is missing
- Ring is broken
- Ring mounting resistance is abnormal
- Camera inspection fails
- Sequence verification fails

## Reaction

- Hold suspected assembly
- Inspect mounting tool
- Verify ring feeder
- Inform quality or supervisor
